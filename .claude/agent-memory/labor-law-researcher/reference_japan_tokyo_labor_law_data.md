# Japan / Tokyo Labor Law Key Data (researched 2026-06)

## Primary Law
- 労働基準法 (Labor Standards Act) — administered by Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare (MHLW)
- Latest major reform cycle: Working Style Reform Act (2019 onwards), 2024–2026 amendments

## Working Hours
- Standard: 40 hrs/week, 8 hrs/day
- Overtime cap (standard 36 Agreement): 45 hrs/month, 360 hrs/year
- Overtime cap (special 36 Agreement): 100 hrs/month, 720 hrs/year; average 2–6 months ≤ 80 hrs/month

## Overtime Premium Rates
- Standard overtime: 125% (25% premium)
- Monthly overtime > 60 hrs: 150% (50% premium) — SME obligation from April 2025
- Statutory holiday work: 135%
- Night work (22:00–05:00): 125% (additive)

## Tokyo Minimum Wage
- ¥1,226/hour from October 3, 2025 (up from ¥1,163 — +63 yen, +5.42%)
- 2026 revision expected autumn 2026; government target national avg ¥1,500 by late 2020s
- Highest regional minimum wage in Japan

## Annual Leave
- 10 days after 6 months continuous service (≥80% attendance)
- Scales up to 20 days after 6.5 years
- Mandatory 5-day minimum annual take (employer obligation since 2019; fine ¥300,000/employee)

## Termination
- 30 days written notice OR 30 days pay in lieu (Article 20, LSA)
- No statutory severance; market practice 3–18 months base salary
- Dismissal abuse doctrine: courts void dismissals lacking objectively reasonable grounds

## Social Insurance (2025 rates, Tokyo)
- Health Insurance (協会けんぽ Tokyo): 9.85% total, split 50/50 employer/employee (from April 2025)
- Nursing Care Insurance (age 40–64): 1.62% total, split 50/50
- Employees' Pension (厚生年金): 18.30% total, split 50/50
- Employment Insurance (雇用保険): 1.55% total (employer 0.95%, employee 0.60%) — general industry
- Workers' Accident Insurance (労災): employer only, rate varies by industry
- Child Rearing Premium: 0.23% from April 2026 (split 50/50)

## Key Recent Amendments
- Oct 2024: Pension enrollment expanded to companies with 51+ employees (part-time workers)
- Apr 2025: SMEs now required to pay 150% for overtime >60 hrs/month; childcare leave expanded
- Apr 2026: Gender pay gap disclosure (101+ employees); medical treatment work-life balance; senior worker accommodations
- Oct 2026: Customer harassment prevention obligations take effect

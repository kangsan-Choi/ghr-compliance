# Netherlands Labor Law Key Data (researched 2026-06)

## Minimum Wage (WML)
- Hourly rate: €14.71/hour (21세 이상, 2026-01-01 기준)
- No fixed monthly rate since 2024; monthly amount depends on contracted hours
- 40-hour week equivalent: ~€2,549.73/month
- Holiday allowance (vakantietoeslag): 8% on top of gross salary — mandatory

## Annual Leave
- Statutory minimum: 4x weekly working hours (주5일 기준 20일)

## Sick Leave
- 최대 2년(104주), 임금의 70% 지급 의무 (1년차: 최저임금 이상 보장)
- Dismissal ban during first 2 years of illness

## Transitievergoeding (Severance)
- Formula: 1/3 month salary per year of service (pro-rated)
- 2026 cap: €102,000 gross (2025: €98,000)
- Entitlement from day 1 of employment

## Social Insurance Employer Contributions 2026
- Max contribution base: €79,409/year
- Aof (disability): 6.27% (small) / 7.63% (large)
- Whk WGA (long-term disability): 0.96%
- Whk ZW (short-term sickness): 0.56%
- Volksverzekeringen (AOW etc.): withheld from employee, employer does not pay

## Probation (Proeftijd)
- Up to 1 month: contracts < 2 years
- Up to 2 months: permanent contracts

## Notice Periods
- Employee tenure-based: 1 month (< 5 years) up to 4 months (≥ 15 years)
- Employer notice double if no UWV/court approval

## Key 2026 Amendments
- False self-employment (Wet DBA) full enforcement since 2026-01-01
- Wet VBAR clarification scrapped 2026-03-06; replaced by Zelfstandigenwet (2027+)
- Legal presumption of employment at <€38/hour — separate legislation by 2026-08-31
- Transitievergoeding UWV compensation: from 2026-07-01 only for <25-employee firms
- 30% ruling stays at 30% in 2026; drops to 27% from 2027-01-01

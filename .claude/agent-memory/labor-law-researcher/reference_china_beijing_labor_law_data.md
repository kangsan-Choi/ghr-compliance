## China (PRC) + Beijing Municipal Labor Law — Key Data (researched 2026-06)

### Minimum Wage (Beijing)
- Monthly: CNY 2,540 (effective 2025-09-01)
- Hourly (part-time): CNY 27.7/hr
- Highest hourly rate among all Chinese provinces

### Social Insurance (Beijing) — 五险一金
- Contribution base: floor CNY 7,162 / ceiling CNY 35,811 (from 2025-07)
- Pension: employer 16%, employee 8%
- Medical (incl. maternity): employer 9.8%, employee 2%
- Unemployment: employer 0.5%, employee 0.5%
- Work injury: employer 0.2–1.9% (industry-based), employee 0%
- Housing fund: employer 12%, employee 12%

### Standard Working Hours
- 40 hrs/week, 8 hrs/day
- Max overtime: 36 hrs/month
- Overtime premium: 150% weekday, 200% rest day, 300% statutory holiday

### Annual Leave (national)
- 1–10 years service: 5 days
- 10–20 years: 10 days
- 20+ years: 15 days

### Sick Leave / Medical Period
- <5 years service: 3 months medical period
- 5–10 years: 6–9 months
- 10–15 years: 12–18 months

### Severance (经济补偿金)
- N = 1 month salary per year of service (capped at 3x regional avg × 12 years)
- N+1 = N + 1 month if no 30-day prior written notice given
- 2N = wrongful/illegal termination

### Probation
- Contract 3m–1yr: max 1 month probation
- Contract 1–3yr: max 2 months
- Contract 3yr+ or indefinite: max 6 months
- Only one probation per employment relationship

### Key Recent Amendments
- 2025-01-01: Retirement age gradual reform takes effect (men 60→63 over 15 yrs; women 55→58 / 50→55)
- 2025-01-01: Spring Festival holiday extended by 1 day (3→4 days)
- 2025-09-01: SPC Judicial Interpretation II on Labor Disputes takes effect — tightens non-compete, clarifies indefinite contract, social insurance mandatory

Key sources for Myanmar labor law research:

- ILO Myanmar publications: https://www.ilo.org/publications/ilo-guide-myanmar-labour-law
- Myanmar MoL laws page: https://mol.gov.mm/laws-and-regulations/
- Labour Organization Law (mol.gov.mm): https://mol.gov.mm/en/the-labour-organization-law/
- Settlement of Labour Disputes Law (DICA): https://www.dica.gov.mm/sites/dica.gov.mm/files/document-files/labour_dispute_settlement_law_2012_eng.pdf
- DICA (investment/company admin): https://www.dica.gov.mm/
- Tilleke & Gibbins Myanmar updates: https://www.tilleke.com/ (reliable law firm for Myanmar)
- DFDL Myanmar: https://www.dfdl.com/ (reliable law firm for Myanmar)
- WageIndicator Myanmar: https://wageindicator.org/labour-laws/labour-law-around-the-world/minimum-wages-regulations/minimum-wages-regulations-myanmar
- Atlas HXM Myanmar minimum wage: https://www.atlashxm.com/resources/myanmar-minimum-wage-increase-august-2024
- SSB Wikipedia: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Social_Security_Board_(Myanmar)
- PoVAW analysis: https://www.womenofburma.org/sites/default/files/2019-06/WLB_POVAW_Analysis_English%20(Final).pdf
- US State Dept Myanmar sanctions: https://ofac.treasury.gov/sanctions-programs-and-country-information/burma
- EU Myanmar sanctions: https://www.consilium.europa.eu/en/policies/sanctions-against-myanmar/
- Progressive Voice labor rights report 2021-2025: https://progressivevoicemyanmar.org/2025/12/04/targeted-abused-exploited-the-erosion-of-labor-rights-in-myanmar-2021-2025/

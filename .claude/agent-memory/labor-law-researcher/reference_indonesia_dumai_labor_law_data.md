## Key Data Points (researched 2026-06)

### Primary Laws
- UU No. 13/2003 tentang Ketenagakerjaan (Manpower Law)
- UU No. 6/2023 (ratification of Perppu No. 2/2022 = revised Omnibus Law)
- PP No. 35/2021 (implementing regulation: PKWT, outsourcing, working hours, termination)
- PP No. 36/2021 (wages, THR)
- PP No. 6/2025 (JKP scheme enhancement)

### Dumai UMK 2026
- Dumai city UMK: IDR 4,431,174 /month (highest in Riau province)
- Riau UMP 2026: IDR 3,780,496 /month
- Basis: Riau Governor's Decree Kpts.1164/XII/2025, signed 23 Dec 2025, effective 1 Jan 2026
- Increase: +7.59% vs 2025 UMK of IDR 4,118,659

### Working Hours
- 40 hrs/week (8h×5d or 7h×6d)
- Max overtime: 4 hrs/day, 18 hrs/week
- Overtime: 1.5x first hour weekday, 2x subsequent hours; different formula for rest/holiday days

### Annual Leave: 12 days after 12 months service

### Maternity/Paternity
- Maternity: 3 months (90 days) paid; extendable 3 more months in special circumstances
- Paternity: 2 days (Manpower Law) + 3 days added by Law No. 4/2024 (KIA Law) = 5 days

### Severance (Pesangon) — PP 35/2021
- 3 components: Uang Pesangon + Uang Penghargaan Masa Kerja + Uang Penggantian Hak
- Pesangon: up to 9 months' wage (based on years of service)
- Long service: 2 months (3 yrs) up to 10 months (24+ yrs)
- Omnibus Law reduced multiplier to 0.5x–1x of statutory amount depending on termination reason

### BPJS Rates (2026)
- BPJS Kesehatan: 5% total (4% employer, 1% employee), capped salary IDR 12,000,000
- BPJS Ketenagakerjaan JKK: 0.24%–1.74% employer (by industry risk)
- JKM: 0.3% employer
- JHT: 3.7% employer + 2% employee (no cap)
- JP: 2% employer + 1% employee; ceiling IDR 11,086,300 (raised March 2026)

### THR
- 1 month salary for 12+ months service; proportional for 1–12 months
- Must be paid in lump sum ≥7 days before religious holiday
- Late payment penalty: 5% of THR amount

### Key Recent Events
- MK Decision No. 168/PUU-XXI/2023 (31 Oct 2024): partially grants judicial review; PKWT capped 5 years total; standalone labor law mandated
- SEMA No. 3/2023 (29 Dec 2023): PKWT early termination = balance of contract pay + compensation
- PP No. 6/2025: JKP (job loss insurance) scheme strengthened
- Law No. 4/2024 (KIA Law): paternity leave expanded to 5 days

# Myanmar Labor Law Key Data (researched 2026-05-15)

## Minimum Wage History
- 2018: MMK 4,800/day (base, still the statutory base)
- Oct 2023: +MMK 1,000 allowance → effective MMK 5,800/day (10+ employee firms)
- Aug 2024: +MMK 1,000 allowance → effective MMK 6,800/day (Notification No. 1/2024)
- Oct 2025: +MMK 1,000 allowance → effective MMK 7,800/day (~USD 3.72)
- NOTE: Daily allowances excluded from overtime calculation base (only MMK 4,800 used)

## Severance Pay (Notification 84/2015 under ESDL 2013)
- 6 months–1 year: 0.5 months
- 1–2 years: 1 month
- 2–3 years: 1.5 months
- (scales up to) 25+ years: 13 months
- Not payable on resignation, fixed-term expiry, or gross misconduct termination

## Notice Period
- Standard: 30 days (1 month) for both employer and employee

## SSB Contributions (Social Security Law 2012)
- Mandatory for 5+ employee firms
- Employer: 3% of monthly salary
- Employee: 2% of monthly salary
- Contribution ceiling: MMK 300,000/month
- Payment deadline: 15th of following month

## Working Hours & Overtime
- Standard: 8 hours/day, 44 hours/week
- Overtime rate: 2x regular hourly wage (Payment of Wages Law 2016)

## Dispute Resolution (Settlement of Labour Disputes Law 2012)
- WCC mandatory for 30+ employee workplaces (4 members: 2 employer + 2 worker)
- 3-tier: WCC → Township Conciliation Body → Arbitration Body → Arbitration Council
- Timeline: 5 days for conciliation attempt, 7 days for decision

## Political Risk (Post-2021 coup)
- 16 labor organizations declared illegal (March 2021)
- 300+ union activists arrested; 55 union leaders reported killed
- US EO 14014 (Feb 2021): OFAC sanctions on military/business interests
- EU restrictive measures renewed through April 2026
- Myanmar NUG (opposition) runs parallel mol.nugmyanmar.org — use SAC mol.gov.mm for operative law

## PoVAW Law Status
- Draft submitted to parliament; as of 2026-05 NOT confirmed enacted under military government
- No explicit workplace sexual harassment law — Penal Code (British-era) is fallback

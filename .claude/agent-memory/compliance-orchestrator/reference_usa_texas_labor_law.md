## 2026 Key Standards
- Minimum wage: $7.25/hour (federal, no separate Texas rate)
- Overtime: 1.5x over 40hrs/week (FLSA, no Texas-specific law)
- Exempt salary threshold: $684/week ($35,568/year) - court blocked 2024 increase
- Social Security: 6.2% employer + 6.2% employee (wage base $184,500)
- Medicare: 1.45% employer + 1.45% employee
- FUTA: 6% on first $7,000 (effective 0.6% after SUTA credit)
- Texas SUTA: new employer 2.7%, RTR 0.21%
- FMLA: 12 weeks unpaid, employer 50+ workers within 75 miles
- Paid sick leave: NO state mandate (city ordinances blocked)
- Paid family leave: NO state/federal mandate for private employers
- Workers comp: VOLUNTARY in Texas (except govt contractors)

## 2025-2026 Key Amendments
- 2024-11-15: Federal court blocked FLSA exempt salary increase nationwide - stays at $684/wk
- 2025-07-04: One Big Beautiful Bill Act - tip tax deduction 2025-2028
- 2026-01-01: TRAIGA (HB 149) - AI governance, prohibits intentional discrimination via AI in employment
- 2026-01-01: SB 1318 - physician/healthcare non-compete strengthened (buyout clause, 1yr, 5-mile)

## Compliance Gap Patterns (Foreign Companies in Texas)
- Misclassification of employees as independent contractors (no benefits)
- Failing to pay overtime to misclassified "exempt" workers below $684/week
- Not maintaining FMLA policy documentation for 50+ employee companies
- Confusion about at-will employment vs. contractual protections

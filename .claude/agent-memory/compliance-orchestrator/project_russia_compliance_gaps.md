## Critical Gaps (High Risk) — Typically Found in Foreign Companies

1. **Salary payment frequency (Ст.136 ТК РФ)** — Foreign companies often pay monthly; Russian law mandates twice monthly. Penalty: up to 100,000 RUB per violation, repeat violation can mean director disqualification 1-3 years.

2. **Overtime base rate calculation (Ст.152, amended 2024-09-01)** — Companies calculating overtime on base salary only are non-compliant since September 1, 2024. All compensatory and incentive allowances must be included. Retroactive correction may be needed.

## Advisory Gaps (Medium Risk)

3. **Redundancy notice period** — Foreign companies commonly set 30-day notice; Russian law requires 60 days for redundancy/liquidation scenarios.

4. **MROT tracking** — Large annual increases (16.5% in 2025, 20.7% in 2026) require proactive salary floor reviews, especially for regional minimums (Moscow, SPb exceed federal MROT significantly).

5. **Annual leave split rule** — At least one continuous block of 14 days required; internal policies often do not explicitly state this.

6. **USC contribution base ceiling** — Must be updated in payroll systems each January; failure to update causes over-withholding or under-reporting.

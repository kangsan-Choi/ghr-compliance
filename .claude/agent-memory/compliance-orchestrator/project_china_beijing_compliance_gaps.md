## Top Risk Areas for Foreign Companies in Beijing

### Critical (Red) Patterns
1. **Social insurance base underreporting** — Common to declare at minimum base (7,162 CNY) regardless of actual salary; labor arbitration risk is high
2. **Open-term contract conversion failure** — After 2 consecutive fixed-term contracts or 10-year tenure; 2025 judicial interpretation tightened anti-avoidance rules
3. **Maternity leave shortfall** — Providing only 98 days (national standard) without Beijing's additional 60-day extension (total should be 158 days)
4. **Minimum wage violation** — Especially part-time and low-skill roles; Beijing raised to 2,540 CNY from Sep 2025
5. **No written contract or delayed signing** — Triggers double salary penalty from month 2

### Advisory (Yellow) Patterns
1. **Overtime documentation gaps** — Monthly 36-hour cap often exceeded; records not maintained
2. **Unused annual leave handling** — Forfeiture policies instead of 300% compensation when employer fails to arrange leave
3. **Probation wage at exactly 80%** — Legal minimum but recommend 85%+ to avoid disputes
4. **Housing provident fund contribution rate** — Many foreign companies set at minimum 5%; employees may request increase

### Emerging Issues (2025-2026)
- Retirement age reform affecting long-tenure employees near retirement
- Cross-border HR data transfers to overseas group HQ — exemption conditions must be documented
- Non-compete agreement validity — compensation payment required; without it, agreement is unenforceable
- Labor dispatch (派遣) ratio compliance — must remain under 10% of total workforce

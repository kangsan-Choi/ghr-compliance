## 2026 Key Standards
- Minimum wage: €14.71/hour (21+, H1 2026, adjusted every 6 months)
- Annual leave: minimum 20 days (4x weekly working days, BW 7:634)
- Holiday allowance (Vakantiegeld): minimum 8% of annual gross salary
- Statutory leave expiry: 6 months after the calendar year granted (July 1 following year)
- Transition payment (Transitievergoeding): 1/3 month per year served, max €102,000 (2026)
- Notice period: 1mo (0-5yr) / 2mo (5-10yr) / 3mo (10-15yr) / 4mo (15yr+)

## Social Insurance Rates (2026)
- ZVW health insurance (employer): 6.51% (max base €75,864)
- WGA disability insurance (employer): avg 0.96% (up from 0.83%)
- ZW sickness benefits (employer): avg 0.56% (up from 0.50%)
- AOW+Anw+WLZ (employee national insurance): ~27.65% (max base €39,296)

## Parental/Maternity Leave
- Maternity: 16 weeks paid (100%, UWV pays up to €304.25/day)
- Partner leave: 1 week 100% + 5 weeks 70% (within 6 months of birth)
- Parental leave: 26 weeks total; first 9 weeks paid at 70% by UWV; 17 weeks unpaid; until child age 8

## 2025-2026 Key Amendments
- 2025-01-01: DBA enforcement resumed - bogus self-employment (schijnzelfstandigheid) actively enforced
- 2026-01-01: Wet minimumloon - hourly minimum wage fully replaces monthly/weekly/daily rates
- 2026-01-01: New CLA for agency workers - equal pay, bonuses, leave, pension; max 3 years
- 2026-01-01: Wtp (Future Pensions Act) - DC pension conversion begins, entry age lowered to 18
- 2026 mid: EU Pay Transparency Directive implementation pending (gender pay gap reporting)
- 2027-01-01: Bogus self-employment fines extended to negligent violations (not just intentional)
- 2027-01-01: Zero-hours contract abolition / mandatory basic contract (legislation pending)

## ZZP Risk Threshold
- ZZP hourly rate below €36 = legal presumption of employee status
- Tax authority actively auditing since 2025-01-01

## Compliance Gap Patterns (Foreign Companies in Netherlands)
- Not paying transition payment on dismissal (common for Korean/Asian companies unfamiliar with Dutch law)
- Failing to provide 8% Vakantiegeld (holiday allowance) separately or in contract
- Using zero-hours contracts without planning for 2027 abolition
- Misclassifying ZZP workers below €36/hr threshold
- Pension non-compliance - not enrolling employees under 25 (now 18 under Wtp)
- Chain of fixed-term contracts exceeding 3 contracts or 3 years (auto-conversion to permanent)

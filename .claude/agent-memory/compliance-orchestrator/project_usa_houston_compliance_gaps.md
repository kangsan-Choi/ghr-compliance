## Key Gap Patterns for Foreign (Korean) Companies in Houston, TX

### Critical (Red) Risk Areas
1. **Exempt/Non-exempt Misclassification** — Foreign companies often apply home-country logic (e.g., all managers = exempt). Must meet BOTH the $684/week salary test AND the duties test. Court blocked 2024 increase; stays at $684/wk as of 2026.
2. **Final Paycheck Timing** — Involuntary termination: 6 calendar days (Texas Payday Law §61.014). Many foreign HR teams default to "next payday" which is a violation for terminated employees.
3. **Non-exempt Monthly Pay Cycle** — Texas Labor Code §61.011 requires semi-monthly minimum for non-exempt workers. Korean companies used to monthly payroll frequently violate this.
4. **I-9 Documentation** — Must complete within 3 business days of hire start. Failure = $2,789+ per violation.
5. **EEO/Anti-discrimination Policy Absence** — 15+ employee companies must have Title VII, ADA, ADEA policies; absence = no defense in discrimination suits.
6. **WARN Act Process Gaps** — 100+ employee companies need 60-day notice for mass layoffs (50+) or plant closings.
7. **COBRA Notification Failure** — 20+ employee companies with health insurance must notify departing employees of COBRA rights within 14 days of qualifying event.

### Advisory (Yellow) Risk Areas
1. **Workers' Comp Non-subscriber Reporting** — Texas is the ONLY state where WC is voluntary. Non-subscribers MUST file annual notice with TDI + written employee notice. Missing the employee notice is common.
2. **PTO/Vacation Policy Not Documented** — No statutory obligation, but once a written policy exists, it is enforceable under Texas Payday Law. Ambiguous policies = litigation risk.
3. **FMLA Policy Not Posted** — 50+ employee companies must post FMLA notice AND include it in employee handbooks.
4. **Comp Time (Comp-off) for Non-exempt** — Legal only for public sector. Private employers giving comp time instead of 1.5x OT = FLSA violation.

### 2026 New Compliance Items
- **TRAIGA (HB 149)** — Effective 2026-01-01. AI tools used in hiring/performance/termination must not intentionally discriminate. Bias audit recommended.
- **SB 1318** — Effective 2025-09-01. Healthcare non-competes: max 1yr, 5-mile radius, buyout cap = annual salary.

**Why:** Houston is a major hub for Korean corporate subsidiaries (energy, manufacturing, logistics). Federal+Texas dual-layer creates confusion. Foreign companies frequently default to home-country HR practices.

**How to apply:** When generating Houston reports, always distinguish federal vs. Texas requirements per item. Flag the dual-layer explicitly. At-will employment and voluntary workers' comp are the two most surprising elements for Korean HR managers.

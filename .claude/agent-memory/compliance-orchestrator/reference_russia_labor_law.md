## Key Articles (ТК РФ)
- Ст.91 — Standard working hours (40h/week)
- Ст.99 — Overtime (сверхурочная работа), annual 120h limit (240h expansion bill pending as of 2024-12)
- Ст.115, 122 — Annual leave minimum 28 calendar days
- Ст.136 — Salary payment twice monthly; first half 16th–end of month, second half 1st–15th next month
- Ст.152 — Overtime pay: 1.5x for first 2h, 2x thereafter; amended 2024-09-01 to include all allowances in base
- Ст.178, 180 — Severance and 60-day notice for redundancy/liquidation
- Ст.255, 256 — Maternity leave 140 days (156 complicated, 194 multiple births); parental leave to age 3
- Ст.261 — Protected categories: pregnant women, mothers of children under 3, single parents under 14
- Глава 34 НК РФ — Unified social contributions (30% up to ceiling, 15.1% above)

## Key Annual Figures (verify each January)
- MROT 2025: 22,440 RUB/month (Federal Law №82-FZ)
- MROT 2026: 27,093 RUB/month (+20.7%, Federal Law №429-FZ dated 2025-11-28)
- Moscow regional minimum 2025: 32,916 RUB; St. Petersburg: 28,750 RUB
- USC contribution base ceiling 2025: 2,759,000 RUB/employee/year
- Overtime pay amendment effective: 2024-09-01 (all compensation included in base rate)
- Salary delay interest: 1/300 CBR refinancing rate per day (amended 2024-01-30)

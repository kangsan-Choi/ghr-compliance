## Key Legal Databases
- National Law Database (NPC): https://flk.npc.gov.cn/
- Beijing Human Resources Bureau: https://rsj.beijing.gov.cn/
- Beijing Housing Provident Fund Center: https://gjj.beijing.gov.cn/

## Key 2025 Beijing Standards
- Monthly minimum wage (effective 2025.9.1): 2,540 CNY/month (up from 2,420)
- Part-time hourly minimum: 27.7 CNY/hr (public holidays: 65.1 CNY/hr)
- Social insurance base: upper 35,811 CNY / lower 7,162 CNY (from July 2025)
- Housing provident fund base: upper 35,811 / lower 2,540 CNY (from 2025.9.1); contribution rate 5-12% each party

## Social Insurance Rates (Beijing 2025)
- Pension: employer 20%, employee 8%
- Medical: employer 6%, employee 2%
- Unemployment: employer 0.8%, employee 0.2%
- Work injury: employer 0.2-1.9%, employee 0%
- Maternity: employer 0.8%, employee 0%
- Total employer burden: ~29.5%; employee: ~10.2%

## Key Laws Referenced
- 劳动法 (Labor Law) Art. 36-44: working hours and overtime
- 劳动合同法 (Labor Contract Law) Art. 10, 14, 19, 20, 39-42, 47, 82, 85, 87
- 职工带薪年休假条例 (State Council Order 514): annual leave
- 女职工劳动保护特别规定: maternity protection
- 北京市人口与计划生育条例 (amended 2021.11.26): 158-day maternity leave (98+60), 15-day paternity
- 社会保险法: social insurance
- 最高人民法院劳动争议司法解释(二) (effective 2025.9.1): open-term contract conversion clarifications
